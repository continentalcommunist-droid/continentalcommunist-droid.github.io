Privacy boundary

The learner database stores an account identifier, display name, time zone, pathway enrollments, completed step identifiers, bookmarks, and notes you deliberately save. It does not collect browsing history or create public profile data. Database policies restrict every learner record, bookmark, and note to its owner.
