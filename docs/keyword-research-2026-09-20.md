## Observed query context

- Annual related-query lists included “is zohran mamdani a socialist,” “democratic socialists of america platform,” and “what is democratic socialism in simple terms.” These identify factual questions, not political preferences or a ranking of people or organizations.
- The 90-day democratic-socialism list included “democratic socialists of america platform,” “whats the difference between socialism and democratic socialism,” and “different types of socialism.” These support separating organization, definition, and terminology pages.
- Recent progressivism results included history-classroom queries such as “suffrage definition us history” and “direct primary definition.” The progressive/liberal guide must state its contemporary American scope and distinguish historical usage.
- Broad “American left” term matching included unrelated American Idol searches; quoted phrase matching also surfaced product searches. It is an audience description, not established evidence of political search volume. Inspect actual query intent before using aggregated demand.
- General socialism/Marxism lists also contained current-event, classroom, and unrelated queries. This worksheet does not commission new content solely because an unrelated query appeared.
