  concept_id: cc.concept.relative-surplus-population
  min_length: 120
  prompt: "Close the source. Explain why the relative surplus population is an internally produced condition of accumulation rather than simply a group left outside capitalism."
  model_answer: "Accumulation expands production and can increase employment, but competition also drives changes in productivity and capital composition that reduce labor required relative to the scale of output. Workers are displaced, drawn in, and expelled unevenly across firms, sectors, regions, and the business cycle. This available labor population disciplines wages and lets capital expand when profitable. It is therefore relative to capital's changing demand and continually reproduced by accumulation, not a fixed population wholly external to capitalist production."
  feedback_points:
  - "Allows accumulation to create jobs as well as displace labor."
  - "Connects productivity or capital composition to reduced labor needs relative to output."
  - "Describes movement across sectors, regions, or phases of the cycle."
  - "Explains the surplus population's role in wage discipline or rapid expansion."
  source_pointer: "Revisit Chapter 25's movement from enlarged capital to changing composition and the different forms of the relative surplus population."

  prompt: "A rural region shifts from customary communal land use to titled parcels acquired by agribusiness, while former users enter seasonal wage work. Analyze this as a possible process of primitive accumulation without assuming the label settles the case."
  model_answer: "The analysis should determine how legal title, debt, taxation, policing, ecological change, or direct force separated users from effective access to land and subsistence. It should trace how land and resources became assets for accumulation while displaced people became available for wage labor. It must also identify resistance, compensation, hybrid livelihoods, and prior market relations. The key question is whether institutions are producing or deepening the separation between producers and means of production, not whether the change merely happened early in history."
  feedback_points:

  - "Looks for resistance, mixed forms, and counterevidence."
