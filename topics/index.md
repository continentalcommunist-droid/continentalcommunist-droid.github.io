## From categories to connected subject hubs

Topics are the connective tissue of the platform. As the archive grows, each hub will bring together work from across Learn, Analysis, Briefings, Library, and Multimedia rather than functioning as a loose tag archive.

The initial controlled vocabulary mirrors the publication's established editorial scope. It can expand deliberately as new bodies of work justify new destinations.
