Eight routes through one library

Each pathway supplies a level, realistic time estimate, prerequisites, scoped readings, written lecture guides, discussion questions, and a working glossary. Longer books use selected chapters unless a complete reading is explicitly assigned.

Private by design

How to use a pathway

Read, interpret, discuss, return.

01

Read with a scope

Assignments identify complete texts or selected chapters so the schedule remains achievable.

02

Use the lecture guides

Each written outline connects the readings and highlights the argument to reconstruct.

03

Test your explanation

Discussion questions and summaries ask you to apply concepts rather than merely recognize terms.

Pathway glossaries

Terms in the context where they matter.

Every pathway closes with six working definitions tied to its readings. The glossaries are deliberately contextual: the same term can carry a more precise meaning as the level of analysis changes.
