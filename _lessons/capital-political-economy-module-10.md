checkpoint:
  item_id: cc.item.capital-political-economy.week-10.retrieval-01
  concept_id: cc.concept.abstract-to-concrete
  min_length: 120
  prompt: "Close the source. Explain the movement from abstract to concrete and why beginning with a visible whole such as 'the economy' can produce a confused analysis."
  model_answer: "A visible whole such as population or the economy contains many relations whose distinct roles have not yet been explained. Analysis first isolates simpler determinations—commodity, value, labor power, capital—without claiming they exist alone. It then reconstructs their connections through production, circulation, competition, credit, classes, states, and the world market. The concrete result is richer because it is a structured unity of determinations, not because description has accumulated more surface detail."
  feedback_points:
  - "Treats the initial visible whole as real but analytically undifferentiated."
  - "Explains abstraction as isolating relations rather than ignoring reality."
  - "Describes the return to the concrete as reconstructing connections among determinations."
  - "Avoids turning the sequence into a universal chronological history."
  source_pointer: "Revisit the Introduction's example of population and the difference between a chaotic conception of the whole and a rich totality of determinations."
worked_example:
  claim: "A hospital's new diagnostic system replaces individual expertise with technology, so knowledge has become independent of labor."
  steps:
  - label: Identify embodied social knowledge
    text: "The system contains accumulated research, clinical classification, software, data work, and prior collective practice."
  - label: Map living labor
    text: "Clinicians, technicians, maintainers, cleaners, data workers, and patients still produce inputs, judgments, and corrections."
  - label: Specify property and control
    text: "Licensing, procurement, professional authority, liability, and management determine access and use."
  - label: Examine the value relation
    text: "The system can reduce task time while budgets, staffing, prices, and competitive pressures continue to organize work through monetary measures."
  - label: Reconstruct the concrete
    text: "Technology is one determination within a system of labor, knowledge, property, institutions, circulation, and social need."
faded_example:
  item_id: cc.item.capital-political-economy.week-10.faded-01
  estimated_minutes: 6
  min_length: 30
  claim: "A hypothetical paper mill installs automated sorting, so technology by itself guarantees profitable growth."
  provided_steps:
  - label: "Begin with simple relations"
    text: "The mill sells paper as a commodity and buys labor power and inputs. Distinguish producing usable paper from realizing value through its sale."
  - label: "Locate embodied social knowledge"
    text: "Its sorting equipment incorporates accumulated engineering and collective experience as fixed capital. The equipment's existence does not yet explain its ownership or use."
  completion_steps:
  - id: connect-production-and-control
    label: "Add labor and control"
    prompt: "Which relations must be added to explain what the equipment does in production?"
    model_answer: "Identify who operates, maintains, and corrects the sorting process; who sets pace and staffing; and who owns the equipment and its design rights. Technical capacity becomes an actual labor process through these relations, with contested effects on hours, skill, and output."
  - id: connect-circulation-and-finance
    label: "Add circulation and finance"
    prompt: "Suppose the equipment is debt-financed and paper remains unsold for months. How does this change the account?"
    model_answer: "Debt payments and tied-up commodity capital can obstruct renewal despite faster sorting. Profitability depends on sales, turnover, input costs, financing terms, and competition as well as production; the machine cannot independently guarantee an expanded money return."
  - id: form-testable-synthesis
    label: "Reconstruct a testable explanation"
    prompt: "State a connected explanation and one finding that would force you to revise it."
    model_answer: "Automation may reduce sorting time, while staffing decisions determine labor use and sales timing determines whether output finances debt and renewal. If measured total labor per usable ton does not fall because maintenance and rework rise, the predicted productivity gain must be revised before attributing growth to the machine."
  feedback_points:
  - "Connects abstract categories to specific labor, property, and control relations."
  - "Explains how production, sale, turnover, and debt affect one another."
  - "Names evidence that could revise a causal claim rather than merely add description."
  source_pointer: "Return to the Grundrisse, the 1857 Introduction on the method of political economy and the Fragment on Machines on fixed capital, social knowledge, and labor time."
application:
  item_id: cc.item.capital-political-economy.week-10.application-01
  concept_id: cc.concept.general-intellect
  min_length: 180
  prompt: "Write a compact analysis of the generative-AI industry using at least five pathway concepts. Include one claim about production, one about circulation or finance, one about labor, and one piece of evidence that could challenge your explanation."
  model_answer: "A strong analysis might begin with AI services as commodities whose production depends on data-center fixed capital, energy, chips, software, and globally coordinated labor. Firms purchase labor power across research, data preparation, maintenance, and support while appropriating socially accumulated knowledge through intellectual-property and platform control. High infrastructure costs, rapid depreciation, credit, and investor expectations shape turnover and competition; subscription sales must realize claims on future surplus. Automation may create relative-surplus-value pressures in user industries without uniformly eliminating labor. Evidence on actual task time, energy and chip bottlenecks, employment, pricing, utilization, revenue, financing, and failed deployments could confirm or challenge each link."
  feedback_points:
  - "Uses at least five concepts as connected relations rather than a list."
  - "Makes distinct claims about production, labor, and circulation or finance."
  - "Includes fixed capital, social knowledge, ownership, or control without making technology autonomous."
  - "Separates technical possibility from realized productivity and profit."
  - "Names evidence that could falsify or materially revise the explanation."
