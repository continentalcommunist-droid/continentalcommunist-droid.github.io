  model_answer: "If productivity rises in industries producing workers' customary means of subsistence, or their inputs, the value of those goods and therefore the value of labor power can fall. Workers may reproduce the equivalent of their labor power in fewer hours. With the working day unchanged, necessary labor contracts and surplus labor expands. A single firm that adopts a superior method first may also capture temporary extra surplus value until the method becomes socially general."
