## Build the standards before opening the forum

Community is part of the long-term architecture, but it is not being presented as an active discussion forum yet. Moderation capacity, clear rules, privacy practices, and an appeal process must exist before open participation begins.

The governing principle will be to moderate conduct rather than factional orthodoxy. That approach follows Continental Communist's commitment to intellectual independence and evidence-based disagreement.
