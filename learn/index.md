## Twelve pathways through one connected library

The library is organized into twelve guided pathways covering Marxist fundamentals, an intensive Marxist-Leninist core curriculum, political economy, Marxist feminism and social reproduction, American institutions, labor history, race and Reconstruction, markets and planning, technology, and global development.

Each pathway includes a level, estimated effort, prerequisites, scoped readings, written lecture guides, discussion questions, a working glossary, and private progress tracking. The first modular lesson pilot adds closed-book retrieval, explanatory feedback, and scheduled follow-up review. An optional learner account synchronizes pathway progress across devices; study without an account remains available.
