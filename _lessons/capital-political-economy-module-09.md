checkpoint:
  item_id: cc.item.capital-political-economy.week-09.retrieval-01
  concept_id: cc.concept.rate-of-profit
  min_length: 120
  prompt: "Close the source. Explain why profit appears to arise from total capital and how that appearance obscures the source of surplus value."
  model_answer: "For the firm, the excess of sale revenue over cost price appears as a return on all capital advanced, including machinery, materials, and wages. The profit rate therefore takes the form s divided by c plus v, and competition further redistributes surplus through prices of production. Because the distinct role of living labor disappears from this accounting relation, profit seems generated by capital as a whole or by the market. The appearance is real for decision-making but conceals surplus value's origin in surplus labor."
  feedback_points:
  - "Relates profit to the firm's total capital advanced and cost price."
  - "Distinguishes s/(c+v) from the exploitation relation s/v."
  - "Acknowledges redistribution through competition or prices of production."
  - "Explains why an objectively useful business form can obscure surplus labor."
  source_pointer: "Revisit Part I's movement from surplus value to profit and note how cost price removes the distinction between c and v from the capitalist's view."
worked_example:
  claim: "A retailer reports a higher margin than a manufacturer, proving retail workers created more surplus value."
  steps:
  - label: Distinguish production from realization
    text: "A reported margin records revenue over accounted cost; it does not directly identify where new value originated."
  - label: Follow redistribution
    text: "Wholesale prices, commercial discounts, rent, logistics, taxes, and market power redistribute portions of total surplus."
  - label: Examine labor in both firms
    text: "Retail labor may perform necessary circulation functions and be exploited without all commercial profit originating there."
  - label: Compare capital and turnover
    text: "Different fixed capital, inventory speed, credit terms, and risks shape observed rates and margins."
  - label: Specify the evidence gap
    text: "Firm accounts alone cannot reconstruct the total value and surplus relations across the chain."
application:
  item_id: cc.item.capital-political-economy.week-09.application-01
  concept_id: cc.concept.credit
  min_length: 140
  prompt: "Low interest rates fuel rapid construction financed by short-term borrowing, then refinancing conditions tighten while buildings remain unfinished. Analyze credit's enabling and destabilizing roles."
  model_answer: "Credit pools money, accelerates investment, and lets construction begin before prior sales have returned capital, coordinating a larger scale of production. It also separates commitments from current cash flows and links developers, banks, contractors, suppliers, and future buyers through repayment schedules. When refinancing tightens, long production times and unfinished commodity capital prevent rapid repayment or sale. Forced asset sales, halted orders, and defaults can transmit the interruption. Credit did not create the underlying value, but it accelerated accumulation and concentrated the consequences of failed realization and timing."
  feedback_points:
  - "Explains how credit mobilizes and coordinates investment before completed turnover."
  - "Maps repayment and refinancing links among several actors."
  - "Connects long production time or unfinished output to liquidity pressure."
  - "Treats credit as an accelerator and transmission mechanism rather than the sole cause of crisis or source of value."
  source_pointer: "Return to Part V and separate interest-bearing claims from the production and realization processes on which repayment ultimately depends."
