## Build outward from the strongest existing resource

The Reading List is the foundation of the Library. It brings Marxist primary texts into conversation with American labor history, political thought, economic development, technology, institutions, and competing traditions.

The next library layer will separate books, texts, people, concepts, and sources into reusable records so they can be connected to learning paths and analysis throughout the site.
