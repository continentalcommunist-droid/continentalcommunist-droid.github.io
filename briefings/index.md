## A briefing practice is being built

Briefings will provide a dependable way to follow events without reproducing the churn of a general-purpose news feed. Each briefing will point back to relevant topics, sources, and longer analysis.

Until a regular cadence is established, this page serves as the durable home for the format and labels the weekly edition as planned.
